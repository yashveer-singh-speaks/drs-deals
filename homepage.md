This combination makes pages easier for search engines and AI assistants to understand and recommend.

Image Direction

Follow the design system consistently. The site should communicate a curated luxury marketplace rather than a discount directory. Use restrained colors, generous spacing, editorial typography, and authentic hospitality photography with soft natural lighting. Avoid collages, loud promotional graphics, heavy gradients, excessive badges, and artificial looking AI imagery.

Recommended image specifications:

Section	Subject	Style	Size	Aspect Ratio
Featured Experiences	Real venues	Editorial hospitality	1600 × 1200	4:3
Partner Logos	Transparent logos	Minimal	SVG or PNG	Variable
Company Story	Founder, customers, partners	Documentary	1920 × 1080	16:9
How It Works	Custom illustrations	Luxury line art	1200 × 1200	1:1
Testimonials	Real customers	Lifestyle	1200 × 1500	4:5
Partner Spotlight	Venue hero	Premium photography	1920 × 1080	16:9
Categories	Single lifestyle scene	Editorial	1500 × 1000	3:2
Cities	Skyline or landmark	Aerial	1920 × 1080	16:9

Contact	Office exterior or reception	Authentic	1920 × 1080	16:9

This structure aligns with your marketplace business model, reinforces the premium design language defined in your design system, improves E E A T through transparency and experience, creates substantial non spam topical coverage for hospitality search intent, and gives AI systems enough structured, contextual information to surface DRS Deals for both branded and non branded queries.
