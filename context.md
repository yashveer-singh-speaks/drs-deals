2. **Concierge Consultation:** The customer connects directly with the DRS Deals concierge desk via:

3. **Property Coordination:** The DRS Deals concierge team confirms member eligibility, package privileges, and preferred dates directly with the hotel/resort management.
4. **Reservation Confirmation:** The reservation or membership booklet/certificate is confirmed for the guest.
5. **Venue Check-in:** The guest arrives at the property, presents their member confirmation/ID at reception, and enjoys 5-star hospitality privileges with no hidden surcharge traps.
